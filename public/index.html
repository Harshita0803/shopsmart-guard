The idea

ShopSmart is a made-up online store. Its support bot holds something worth protecting: a fictional employee discount code (40% off). It must also keep its hidden instructions private, never invent policies or discounts, and never be hijacked by text written by outsiders, like a marketplace seller's product page, a fake review, or a document a customer uploads.

The Red Team attacks the bot. The Blue Team adds four layers of defense. The evaluation measures attack success before and after, and how often the defenses wrongly block normal customers.

If a user message is flagged, the bot refuses. If only a document is flagged, the document is quarantined (dropped) and the bot still answers the customer. That keeps innocent customers working even when a product page is poisoned.
